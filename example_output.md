### 1. Benefit Eligibility & Diagnostic Requirements

* **Eligibility:** Because you are 65 or older and enrolled in Medicare Part B, you qualify under the statutory lymphedema compression benefit.
* **Qualifying Diagnosis:** Your condition maps to post-mastectomy secondary lymphedema (**ICD-10: I97.2**).
* **Clinical Requirement:** Garments must be prescribed by your treating physician/practitioner and dispensed by a Medicare-enrolled DMEPOS supplier.

---

### 2. Covered Items & HCPCS Billing Codes

Your authorized benefit covers gradient compression items isolated under Medicare's operational billing categories:

* **Daytime Custom or Standard Arm Sleeves:** HCPCS **A6583** / **A6580**
* **Daytime Custom or Standard Gloves/Gauntlets:** HCPCS **A6584** / **A6581**
* **Nighttime Compression Garments:** HCPCS **A6589**
* **Bandaging & Padding Supplies:** Applicable sub-bandage wraps and foam pads used during active decongestive therapy.

---

### 3. Utilization & Replacement Caps

Under CMS operational directives, the following frequency limits apply:

* **Daytime Garments:** Up to **3 garments per affected limb every 6 months**.
* **Nighttime Garments:** Up to **2 garments per affected limb every 2 years**.

---

### 4. Regional Pricing & Coinsurance (Wyoming)

* **Adjudication Jurisdiction:** Processed under DME MAC Jurisdiction D (Noridian) using the **Wyoming DMEPOS Fee Schedule**.
* **Reimbursement Rates (Wyoming Fee Schedule):**
* *HCPCS A6583 (Custom Arm Sleeve):* Wyoming fee schedule ceiling allows approximately **$148.20** per unit.
* *HCPCS A6584 (Custom Glove):* Wyoming fee schedule ceiling allows approximately **$172.50** per unit.


* **Out-of-Pocket Expense:** Standard Medicare Part B cost-sharing applies—Medicare covers **80%** of the allowed amount, and you are responsible for the **20% coinsurance** after your annual Part B deductible has been met.
