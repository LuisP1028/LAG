You have the exact sequence, with one small correction on where the cross-references live:

1. **Step 1 (Legislation):** Discovers all bills and the enacted law. Its **Bill Numbers** (e.g., H.R. 3630) are the keys used to discover the **Lobbying Groups** (via Senate Lobbying Disclosures) and CBO economic scorecards.
2. **Step 2 (Deltas):** Discovers the line-by-line changes made to permanent statute (Title 42 of the U.S. Code).
3. **Step 3 (Agency Execution):** Uses those statutory deltas to find the agency (CMS), its regulations (42 CFR), operational transmittals, and billing codes (HCPCS & ICD-10).
4. **The Clinical Evidence (PubMed):** The **PubMed articles** are cited in **Step 3** (inside the CMS Final Rule's preamble to clinically justify compression grades and coverage limits), rather than Steps 1 or 2.

Every record in Layer 4 is unlocked by a direct foreign key handed to you by Step 1 or Step 3.
